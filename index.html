Explore Darjeeling

Welcome to Darjeeling!

Darjeeling is a beautiful hill station located in the Indian state of West Bengal. It is known for its tea plantations, stunning views of the Himalayas, and vibrant culture. Visitors can enjoy a variety of activities including trekking, visiting monasteries, and exploring the local markets.

Places to Visit

  • Darjeeling Himalayan Railway
  • Batasia Loop
  • Tiger Hill
  • Peace Pagoda
  • Tea Gardens

Things to Experience

  1. Ride the Toy Train
  2. Watch the Sunrise at Tiger Hill
  3. Visit the Monasteries
  4. Explore the Local Markets
  5. Enjoy a Cup of Darjeeling Tea

Why Visit Darjeeling?

Darjeeling is a paradise for nature lovers and tea enthusiasts alike. With its lush green tea gardens, breathtaking views of the Himalayas, and rich cultural heritage, it offers a unique blend of natural beauty and spiritual tranquility.
